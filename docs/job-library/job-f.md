## Lessons for Project Check
- Even an open, uninsulated loading canopy needed full planning here.
- Expect conditions on lighting and ground vibration. Mining-area sites need a mining risk review.
- A gap of 330 mm to an existing wall raises fire-spread and maintenance-access questions to check.
- Ordering before the planning decision is a risk. Highways was the last consultee to reply.
- The ridge height differed between the quote and the workshop drawing. Check it matches the planning drawings.
