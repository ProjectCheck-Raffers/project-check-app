## Lessons for Project Check
- An open-fronted, demountable canopy was still taken through full planning.
- The length changed from 20 m to 17 m between quotes. Keep the planning drawings in step with the latest quote.
- An early layout about 2 m from the boundary needs an external fire spread check before quoting.
- A translucent PVC roof needs its fire classification on file.
- Check dates on quotes (one was dated 1926 by mistake).
