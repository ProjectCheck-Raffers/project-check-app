# Reference planning cases (public planning documents, not supplier builds)

Anonymised. Kept as examples of what planners and consultees asked for.

| Ref | Region | Case |
|---|---|---|
| R1 | Central Scotland | Bonded whisky maturation warehouse, about 86 × 60 m, in 5 cells of 999 m². Roller doors with canopies, steel emergency exit doors, ridge and low/high-level ventilation. Supporting pack: desk study, **coal mining risk assessment**, unexploded-ordnance map, drainage strategy **with an attenuation tank**, flood emergency plan and noise report. Decision UNKNOWN. |
| R2 | Central Scotland | Whisky storage facility. Drawings marked approved (8 bays of about 4.6 m, brick facade). Decision date UNKNOWN. |

## Lessons
- Large storage buildings in flood-risk or coal-mining areas need a full technical pack.
- Scotland has its own forms and system.
