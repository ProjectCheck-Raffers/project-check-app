## Lessons for Project Check
- Any increase in height after planning needs a variation (or a new application) **before** building.
- Even a small extension next to an existing building and boundary got a full Part B, BR 187 and Part M request from Building Control.
- Most defects came from customer-scope work: slab, falls and drainage at the joins.
- Don't plan a start date before the consents are in hand.
