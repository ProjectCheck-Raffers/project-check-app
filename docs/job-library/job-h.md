## Lessons for Project Check
- Even a 133 m² unit needed 60-minute walls on the two boundary sides.
- Roof PV adds load and planning interest.
- Planners asked for noise, vehicle access and biodiversity net gain evidence.
- Highways can defer a small scheme over parking-bay sizes and a lost pedestrian route.
- Moving a door after consent needed a non-material amendment. Fire-door position can be driven by the parking layout.
- Near an airport, crane notification applies.
- One method statement was copied from a template with the wrong scope text. Check before issuing it.
