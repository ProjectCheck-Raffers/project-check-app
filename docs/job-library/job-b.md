## Lessons for Project Check
- Ask for the boundary distance on every elevation. Here one wall needed fire-rated mineral wool and the other three stayed standard PIR.
- An unheated store was quoted with U 0.54 walls. A Building Control pass for that isn't shown in the documents.
- The number of fire exits changed between the quote and the build (8 to 3). Fix exits from the fire strategy, not the quote.
- The client's purchase terms put the regs-compliance risk on the supplier.
