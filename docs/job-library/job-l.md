# Job L – 1,000 m² spray-room building, South East England (2026 quote)

Anonymised record. Quote stage.

| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Building | Insulated steel (IPE columns, lattice roof) for a spray room. One gable built as an intermediate frame so the building can be extended later. |
| Size | 20 × 50 m, **10 m eaves**, 11.76 m ridge, 10° pitch. Snow 0.47 kN/m², wind 0.88 kN/m². |
| Cladding | Roof: 80 mm panel quoted at "U 0.31". Walls: 60 mm panel quoted at "U 0.23". **These values look swapped or wrong for the thicknesses, so they need checking.** |
| Doors and windows | 2 roller doors 6 × 6 m, 5 push-bar fire doors (U 1.4) and 20 windows. |
| Services | Distribution board, 300 lux high-bay lighting and 1 socket were quoted. |
| Foundations (customer scope) | Slab 51 × 21 m, **29 pad foundations** sized from the ground report, gas-membrane DPM and 200 mm PAV2 slab. The site investigation put the site **inside a coalfield ground-gas consultation zone**. |
| Heating, planning, Building Control, order | UNKNOWN. |

## Lessons for Project Check
- Check quoted U-values against panel thickness. Here they didn't look right.
- A process use (spraying) may change fire and ventilation needs, so get professional support.
- Ground-gas or coalfield screening drove the membrane and pad design.
