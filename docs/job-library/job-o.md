## Lessons for Project Check
- In Wales, drainage needs separate SAB approval.
- Former mine sites bring contaminated-land checks. Rural sites bring ecology issues and objections.
- Claims in a planning statement must be accurate, because objectors check them.
- Door scope changed between the quote and the build. Check doors and fire exits again before building.
- Roller doors need a 3-phase supply on site.
