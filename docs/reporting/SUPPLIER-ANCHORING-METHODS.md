# Supplier anchoring methods

Official supplier material checked 12 November 2026. The comparison describes the systems covered by each source. Use it to identify options and ask for evidence. A supplier description cannot establish suitability for a customer site. Each paid recommendation requires relevant site evidence, structural reactions, ground or slab capacity, anchor design and competent professional assessment.

| Supplier | Published methods and conditions | Official sources |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Herchenbach | Driven ground spikes through base plates for suitable ground build-ups. Heavy-duty dowels into an adequate reinforced slab, strip foundations or individual footings. Ballasted anchor plates where ground anchoring is unavailable. Pre-assembly checks cover geometry, levels, buried services and pull-out. Supplier examples describe around 800 mm of combined layers for spikes and around 200–300 mm reinforced slabs for some dowel installations. Treat these figures as supplier examples only. | [Ground spikes or dowels? Find the best warehouse anchoring](https://herchenbach-industrial.com/en/company/blog/detail/ground-spikes-or-dowels-find-the-best-warehouse-anchoring), [Accessories for lightweight buildings](https://herchenbach-industrial.com/de/leichtbauhallen/zubehoer), [How a Temporary Warehouse Project Works](https://herchenbach-industrial.com/us/company/blog/detail/how-a-herchenbach-temporary-warehouse-project-works) |
| Lauralu | Flat, stable concrete, tarmac or compacted hardcore according to system and site. Lauralu describes ground stakes and chemically fixed anchors for temporary canopies. Site assessment determines anchoring, drainage and any additional groundworks. Confirm the fixing and substrate for the proposed building. | [Frequently Asked Questions On Temporary Buildings](https://lauraluindustry.co.uk/lauralu-faqs/), [What Are the Benefits of a Lauralu Temporary Canopy?](https://lauraluindustry.co.uk/blog/what-are-the-benefits-of-a-temporary-lauralu-canopy/) |
| Smart-Space | Bolts or spikes for relocatable temporary buildings. The comparison guide describes a level concrete pad or base for interim systems, possible concrete landing pads on tarmac depending on geology, and footings for its permanent steel option. | [Temporary buildings](https://smart-space.co.uk/buildings/temporary-relocatable-industrial-tents/), [Compare Temporary Building Types](https://smart-space.co.uk/buildings/) |
| Aganto | Base plates and ground anchors on suitable firm, reasonably level asphalt, concrete or gravel. Retail guidance also mentions hardcore and specifies resin or toggle anchors according to substrate. Its foundation guidance describes uplift testing for the aluminium systems covered and identifies where additional base or groundworks may be needed. | [Temporary building FAQ](https://www.aganto.co.uk/temporary-buildings-faqs), [Temporary commercial buildings](https://www.aganto.co.uk/temporary-buildings/retail), [What foundations are needed for a temporary building?](https://www.aganto.co.uk/news-insights/what-foundations-are-needed-for-a-temporary-building) |
| Instant Space | Steel base plates with driven steel ground pins, stated as approximately 1 m minimum, or stud anchors. The brochure illustrates a pin for paving/gravel or asphalt and a dowel into a foundation. The supplier markets installations without traditional foundations on suitable firm, level sites, subject to the site, building size and use. | [Materials and Technical Specifications](https://instant-space.co.uk/materials-and-technical-specifications/), [Technology – How Does It Work?](https://instant-space.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/BROCHURE-2016.pdf), [Temporary Warehouses](https://instant-space.co.uk/product/temporary-warehouses/) |
