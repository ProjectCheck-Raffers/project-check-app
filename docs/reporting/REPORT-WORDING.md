# Report wording

Use these examples for the matching evidence state. Replace placeholders with traceable project references.

- **Customer-stated fact.** “The customer reports a concrete slab in the proposed building area.”
- **Intention.** “The customer intends to use the existing slab for the new building.”
- **Unverified base.** “The customer has reported an existing concrete slab and intends to use it for the proposed building. Its construction, condition and suitability for supporting and anchoring the building have not yet been verified.”
- **Verified suitable.** “The structural assessment [reference, revision and date] confirms the existing base is suitable for the proposed building and fixing design, subject to [conditions].”
- **Insufficient evidence.** “The available evidence does not establish that the existing base and proposed fixings can resist the building loads. [Investigation] is required before reuse can be confirmed.”
- **Verified unsuitable.** “The assessment [reference] found that the proposed reuse does not meet [requirement] because [finding].”
- **Heated working environment.** “The customer requires a heated working environment. An insulated building envelope will therefore be required. The final wall, roof, floor and door specification must be confirmed against the operating requirements and relevant verified evidence.”
- **Unheated ambient storage.** “The customer has selected unheated ambient storage and has not specified a maintained internal temperature. Any product or condensation-control needs are assessed separately.”
- **Customer-specified temperature.** “The customer has specified [temperature] °C as the internal target. The envelope and temperature-control systems must be assessed against that target and the proposed use.”
- **Below 2°C.** “The stated internal target is below 2°C. Confirm whether the proposed building system can maintain these conditions and whether specialist cold-store construction and temperature control are required.”
