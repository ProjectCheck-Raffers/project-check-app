# Report decision rules

For foundations and anchoring, record what the customer described, their intention to reuse it, verified findings, unknowns, investigation or design still needed, and any verified recommendation. Record base and anchor suitability separately. A reuse preference gives the project team an option to assess. Keep suitability unverified until evidence and competent assessment support it.

| Customer answer | Report preparation |
| --- | --- |
| Heated working environment | Carry an insulated envelope as a design requirement. Confirm the final specification against the operating requirements and project evidence. |
| Unheated ambient storage | No maintained internal temperature is stated. Assess product needs, moisture and condensation separately before drawing an insulation conclusion. |
| Other temperature requirement | Record the stated internal target in °C and assess the envelope and temperature control against that target. Keep a missing target open. |

A stated target **below 2°C** triggers specialist review. Standard modular prefabricated construction may struggle to achieve or maintain it. Check the need for specialist wall, roof, floor and door construction, vapour and condensation control, and temperature-control equipment. The threshold is a Project Check screening rule. Confirm performance for the particular system and use.
