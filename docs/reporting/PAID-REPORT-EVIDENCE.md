# Paid-report evidence

## Existing base and anchoring

Check the exact building footprint and use (temporary, interim or permanent), relevant slab or foundation drawings, thickness, reinforcement, concrete condition and strength, joints, edges, levels and tolerances, drainage and buried services. Obtain ground investigation or bearing evidence where relevant. Match the proposed structure's support reactions, including uplift, to assessed ground or slab capacity and the proposed anchor design, including edge distance and embedment. Obtain pull-out or uplift tests where the competent assessor calls for them.

## Temperature and envelope

Confirm use, occupancy, product or process limits, the maintained temperature or range, heating or cooling method, internal heat gains and door-opening frequency. Assess wall and roof thermal performance, floor insulation, doors, air leakage, condensation and vapour control. Seek specialist cold-store design where the intended conditions require it.

## Carry evidence into the report

For each material conclusion, record the source, revision, relevant page or drawing reference, site or building association, verification status and limits. Distinguish customer input, supplier literature and project-specific assessment. Where evidence is incomplete, report the gap and the next investigation. Keep the free summary neutral and reserve detailed, evidence-supported solutions and recommendations for the paid report.
