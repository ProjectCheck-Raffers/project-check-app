# End-user profile

Source reviewed 12 November 2026: Prospect_Tracker_UnderReview.xlsx, Leads! worksheet, Job Function column and relevant contact notes. The tracker mixes inbound enquiries, outreach, quoted work and follow-ups. Job functions are incomplete, so treat these as examples of contacts involved in building enquiries rather than a ranking or a list of confirmed initial enquirers.

Roles observed include directors and owners, operations managers, site managers, warehouse supervisors, project managers and procurement managers. Contact names or notes also identify facilities, logistics and production roles, including a logistics manager who requested a quote, a facilities manager identified as the contact for expansion, and a production director mentioned in a handover. The recorded managing director reference includes a decision-maker receiving a quote through another contact.

Use plain operational language. Ask contacts what is on site, what they want to use and how they need the building to operate. Leave structural, anchoring and thermal design checks to the evidence review. This profile carries forward role findings only.
