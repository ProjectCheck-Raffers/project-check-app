# Preliminary technical rules

## Temperature and envelope

Building use and the required internal condition inform heating and the likely envelope. Heated ambient working space calls for an insulated-envelope assumption at concept stage, subject to review. Unheated ambient storage can still need insulation or condensation control depending on the goods, use and exposure. A stated temperature is the customer's requirement in °C. Below 2°C is a Project Check specialist-review flag. A competent assessment must establish the envelope, floor, doors, vapour control and temperature-control details.

## Surface, layout and support

The visible surface describes the site. Structural reuse depends on the building position, reactions (downward, uplift and lateral), proposed anchorage, concrete thickness and reinforcement, subbase, service locations and any additional concentrated loads such as racking. Asphalt in particular warrants checking construction below it and the effect of sustained concentrated loads. A newly designed base still needs project-specific design and evidence.

Levels, gradients and drainage affect positioning and water management. Perimeter sealing, internal or external seals and local bunding or upstands can manage water and require inspection and maintenance. Their presence does not establish structural capacity. Retaining verified hardstanding with targeted work can be commercially preferable to full replacement.

Record potential approaches such as surface anchorage to verified concrete, local pads or thickening, and a new engineered base. Supplier-specific claims, alternative temporary-system anchors, and suitability for other materials need evidence and competent verification. See [Project lessons](PROJECT-LESSONS.md).

## Geometry, openings and design limits

Record length, width, clear height and selected roof pitch. The current concept ridge uses the required clear height as a provisional eaves datum plus half the span times the tangent of the pitch. Actual clear height and eaves height can differ with haunches, equipment and structure. The current steel form uses a 10° default, with 5°–22° input range. This is a product drawing input range.

Clear-span requirements, usable height, racking, span, roof form and local wind/snow conditions influence frame form and bay spacing. Historical portal/truss and bay-width heuristics need a confirmed design basis before use as rules. Retain vehicle use and the required clear opening. Existing indicative door defaults are editable and require project verification. Ordinary personnel-door counts are independent of final means-of-escape design. Travel distances, exits, separation, fire performance and access depend on the actual use, layout and applicable assessment.

Site Layout and Building Design are preliminary context and geometry. The free check identifies evidence gaps without certifying the base, support reactions, envelope, drainage, structural design or statutory compliance.
