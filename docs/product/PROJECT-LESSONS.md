# Lessons from Project Check projects

Sources are the project summaries already recorded in the repository and earlier Project Check evidence review. The examples illustrate checks to make on a new project.

| Project | Evidence recorded | Lesson and limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Job E | Existing concrete lorry park reused. Building position, levels and drainage were checked, with extra sealing on the side with a known fall. | Check the actual footprint and water path. A working seal on one site says nothing about another slab's capacity. |
| Job F | Installation record describes concrete working ground, marked services, site inspection and setting-out levels. The canopy planning decision is separate evidence. | Obtain site information early. Planning evidence does not establish services or slab suitability. Exact historical address aliases need checking against the relevant document. |
| Job B | The recorded proposal was a tall racked warehouse on a lorry park crossed by drains. Ground suitability was unresolved early and later found unsuitable for the intended proposal. | Check racking loads, support positions and drainage before commitment. Keep the confidential tender outside this work. |
| Job C buildings 1 and 2 | Recorded civils proposal retained much of the slab and used a reinforced perimeter thickener. Assembly drawings establish the later structure. | Targeted strengthening is a possible route. Project drawings and project-specific thickening dimensions do not prove reuse elsewhere. |
| Job A | Survey and proposed levels, existing drainage, utility survey and CCTV were recorded. Older information was marked not for construction and later records contained approximate, blocked or inaccessible features. | Check revision, location, coverage and quality before relying on a route or level. |
| Job I | The planning decision identifies the approved planning drawing set. | Keep planning approval separate from utility, drainage, topographical and slab evidence. |
| Job D | The selected reaction schedule includes downward, uplift and lateral load cases for the specific steel building. | Obtain the actual reactions for the proposed structure before assessing bases or anchors. |
| Job L | Earlier evidence review recorded an intrusive concrete investigation and extraction limits for a scanned file. Separately reconstructed examples were used to exercise conflict handling. | Check original source, extraction quality and consistency. Reconstructed test values do not establish site facts. |
